## Portal Structure

The application separates weekly study from comprehensive mock testing via top-level mode navigation:

1. **Week-by-Week Portal:**
   - Dedicated week switcher for all course weeks (Weeks 0 to 12).
   - **Week 0:** Dedicated Assignment 0 diagnostic MCQs (10 questions).
   - **Weeks 1 to 12:** Strictly 3 focused pages per week:
     - **Study Notes:** High-yield concept reminders, core definitions table, and last-minute checklists.
     - **Assignment MCQs:** 10 official assignment questions with instant feedback and shuffle option.
     - **Extra Practice MCQs:** 20 newly written practice questions with detailed rationales and shuffle option.

2. **All-Weeks Quiz (Separate Standalone Mode):**
   - Independent full-course assignment quiz mode (completely separated from weekly navigation).
   - Contains all official assignment questions across all weeks: **130 questions** across Weeks 0 to 12 by default.
   - Built-in filter buttons to toggle between **All Weeks (130)**, the standard **Weeks 1-12 (120)** pool, or any individual week (**W0** through **W12**).
   - Shuffle mode toggle (`Shuffle: ON / OFF`) and `Reshuffle` button for simulated exam conditions.
   - Live score counter, instant feedback, and answer reveals.

3. **Extra Questions Practice (Separate Standalone Mode):**
   - Full bank of **240 exam-style practice questions** with detailed explanations across Weeks 1 to 12.
   - Week-wise filter selector (`All Weeks (240)` or individual weeks).
   - Shuffle mode toggle (`Shuffle: ON / OFF`) and `Reshuffle` button.

4. **Laws & Principles (Separate Standalone Mode):**
   - Comprehensive high-yield reference pack for ecology, biodiversity, and sustainability laws, named rules, population formulas, and governance principles.
   - Organized into 7 focused sections:
     - **8 Must-Know Ecological Laws** (Liebig, Shelford, Gause, Darwin-Wallace, Mendel, Hardy-Weinberg, Lindeman) with scientists, core concepts, application examples, and MCQ clues.
     - **12 Named Ecological Rules** (Bergmann, Allen, Gloger, Jordan, Rapoport, Species-Area, Island Biogeography, Ecological Release, Allee Effect, Founder Effect, Bottleneck Effect, Mass-Energy Conservation).
     - **Population & Community Ecology Principles** with mathematical growth models (Exponential J-curve vs. Logistic S-curve, Lotka-Volterra, r/K selection, etc.).
     - **Biodiversity & Evolution Principles** (Species concepts, genetic drift, inbreeding depression, genetic rescue, formal Myers hotspot criteria, MVP, Mitigation Hierarchy).
     - **Sustainability Principles** (Precautionary, Polluter Pays, Prevention, Intergenerational/Intragenerational Equity, CBDR, Tragedy of the Commons, IPAT, Natural Capital, EIA, LCA).
     - **Interactive Scientist-to-Principle Match Key** with click-to-reveal toggle.
     - **5 Interactive Quick Practice MCQs** with instant grading, explanations, and quiz reset.

## Content Sources and Academic Transparency

- **Official Assignment Questions (130 MCQs across Weeks 0–12):**
  - Transcribed directly from the weekly course assignment documents and solutions PDFs.
  - Where source solution PDFs did not include a printed answer key (such as Week 12), the marked answers and explanations have been identified and inferred from course syllabus concepts and option wording.
- **Extra Practice Questions (240 MCQs across Weeks 1–12):**
  - Supplementary, newly written practice questions and study aids designed to test conceptual understanding and application of course topics (these are not official course exam questions).
- **High-Yield Notes and Reminders:**
  - Curated summaries based directly on the weekly lecture topics and study packs.
